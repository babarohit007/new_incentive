Privacy Policy — ShopDeck Incentive Portal

Last updated: 7 September 2026

ShopDeck Incentive Portal ("the app") is an internal tool for ShopDeck and Blitzscale employees. It lets an employee sign in with their own Google account to view incentive figures computed from a set of internal Google Sheets. This page explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and what happens to it.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in, the app requests permission for:

  • Your basic Google profile — name, email address, and profile picture — to identify you and confirm you're a ShopDeck or Blitzscale employee.
  • Read-only access to Google Sheets (spreadsheets.readonly) — used only to read the specific incentive-tracking spreadsheets the app is configured to display. The app never requests write access and cannot modify any sheet.

How your data is used

Your profile information is used solely to confirm your identity and display it back to you inside the app (e.g. "Signed in as you@shopdeck.com"). The Sheets data you can access is exactly what your own Google account already has permission to see — the app doesn't grant, expand, or bypass any Google Sheets permission; it simply reads what you're already allowed to read and displays it, along with derived incentive calculations.

Where your data is stored

Nowhere but your own browser. The app has no backend server or database — it's a static website. Your Google sign-in and the sheet data it fetches stay entirely within your browser session and are discarded when you sign out or close the tab. Nothing is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the app's developers.

Sharing with third parties

The app does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party access settings .

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to kickstarter@blitzscale.co.
